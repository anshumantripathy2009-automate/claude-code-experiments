# Qualification Framework (NoirFlow BANT-C)

**TL;DR:** Before spending time on any lead, qualify them against Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline, and Character. Bad-fit clients cost 10x more to serve than they pay.

## Why Qualification Matters

Every unqualified prospect who becomes a client = 40 hours of your life + damaged confidence + missed opportunity cost. Learning to say NO to bad leads is the most valuable sales skill you'll build.

## The NoirFlow BANT-C Framework

Standard sales use BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline). We add C (Character) because Indian SMB market has unique behavioral risks.

### B — Budget
Can they afford Standard tier (₹9,999 + ₹1,999/mo)?

**Green flags:**
- Already spending on marketing (Google Ads, Instagram promos, portal listings)
- Business ≥ 1 year old with visible revenue signals
- Multiple staff members

**Red flags:**
- "Free mein karke do bhai"
- "Baad mein pay karenge"
- No visible business activity

### A — Authority
Is this person the decision-maker?

**Green flags:**
- Owner-operator
- Directly signs off on marketing spend
- Available for a 30-min call themselves

**Red flags:**
- "Bhaiya ko puchna hoga"
- "Family business, sab log discuss karenge"
- Third-party manager reaching out

### N — Need
Do they have a REAL problem we solve?

**Green flags:**
- Actively losing customers to competitors with better online presence
- Overwhelmed by manual WhatsApp inquiries
- No online presence at all
- Wants to expand / scale but can't handle inbound

**Red flags:**
- "Just want a website" (no clear reason why)
- "For personal branding" (unless they're a real service pro)
- Vanity-driven, not revenue-driven

### T — Timeline
Are they ready to move NOW?

**Green flags:**
- "Can we start next week?"
- "How fast can you deliver?"
- Active pain point they want solved

**Red flags:**
- "Maybe next quarter"
- "Just gathering info"
- "Will decide later"

### C — Character
Are they respectful, decisive, and reasonable?

**Green flags:**
- Prompt replies
- Asks smart questions
- Respects your time
- Treats you as a partner, not a servant

**Red flags:**
- Rude tone in DMs
- Ghosting between messages
- Making promises they don't keep ("I'll pay tomorrow" for 3 weeks)
- Trying to negotiate before scope is agreed
- Talking down to you due to age

**If a lead fails on Character — walk away regardless of budget.** Toxic clients kill agencies.

## Qualification Scorecard (Score Every Lead)

Rate 1–5 on each dimension. Total /25.

| Dimension | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| Budget | /5 |
| Authority | /5 |
| Need | /5 |
| Timeline | /5 |
| Character | /5 |
| **Total** | **/25** |

- **20–25:** Perfect ICP — pitch aggressively
- **15–19:** Solid lead — qualify further on discovery call
- **10–14:** Borderline — send basic pitch + link, don't spend calls
- **< 10:** Disqualify politely and move on

## Qualification Questions (Ask These BEFORE Booking a Call)

Never book a discovery call cold. Ask these 3 questions in DM first:

1. **"What does your business do, and where are you based?"** (Confirms ICP fit + city)
2. **"How do most customers find you today?"** (Reveals current marketing + digital sophistication)
3. **"What's the #1 growth blocker you're trying to solve?"** (Reveals real need or vanity motive)

If they answer thoughtfully → book discovery call.
If they answer vaguely → send portfolio link, follow up in 7 days.
If they ignore the question and ask "kitna" only → they're a price-shopper, low chance of close.

---

## Politely Disqualifying

Never burn bridges. Every "no" today could be a referral tomorrow.

**Script:**
> "Hey [Name], really appreciate you reaching out. Based on what you shared, I don't think NoirFlow is the right fit right now — you might get more value from [Wix / Canva / free tool]. If your needs grow later, always happy to chat. Best of luck! 🙏"

## Red Flag Client Types (Auto-Disqualify)

1. **The Endless Negotiator** — everything must be 50% off
2. **The Feature Creep** — "just one more thing" every day
3. **The Ghosting Client** — replies once a week
4. **The Micromanager** — wants hourly updates on 5-day project
5. **The Family Discount Seeker** — "bhaiya, dost hoon"
6. **The Portfolio Hunter** — only wants to see your work, never buys
7. **The Ideators** — "I have this idea, tell me if it'll work" — free consulting hunters
