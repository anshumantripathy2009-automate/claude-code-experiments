# Pipeline Tracking

**TL;DR:** Track every prospect from DM to close in a Google Sheet or Airtable. Update within 1 hour of every interaction. What isn't tracked won't close.

## Why Pipeline Matters

At any point, you'll have 20–50 prospects at different stages. Human memory can't handle this. A pipeline system is the difference between a founder and a chaos-driven freelancer.

## Pipeline Stages (NoirFlow Sales Funnel)

```
LEAD → CONTACTED → REPLIED → QUALIFIED → CALL_BOOKED → CALL_HAD → PROPOSAL_SENT → CLOSED_WON / CLOSED_LOST
```

### Stage Definitions

- **LEAD:** Identified prospect, no contact yet
- **CONTACTED:** Cold DM/email sent
- **REPLIED:** Any response (positive or negative)
- **QUALIFIED:** Passed BANT-C, worth spending time
- **CALL_BOOKED:** Discovery call scheduled
- **CALL_HAD:** Discovery call completed
- **PROPOSAL_SENT:** Written proposal delivered
- **CLOSED_WON:** Signed + paid
- **CLOSED_LOST:** Explicit no or ghosted 30+ days

## Recommended Tool

**Google Sheets or Airtable** — free, mobile-accessible, both founders can edit.

## Pipeline Sheet Structure

| # | Date Added | Name | Business | Persona | City | Instagram | Phone | Stage | Last Contact | Next Action | Owner | Notes |

## Pipeline Rules

1. Every new prospect added within 1 hour of first contact
2. Stage updated within 1 hour of every interaction
3. "Next Action" always filled with a date
4. Owner = which founder is running this deal (never both)
5. Weekly Friday review of every open deal

## Weekly Pipeline Review (Friday, 30 min)

Both founders sit together. Go through EVERY open deal:

For each:
- What stage?
- What's the next action?
- Is it stuck? If so, why?
- Should we push, wait, or kill?

Update all statuses. Log week's outcomes.

## Pipeline Metrics to Track

- **Total pipeline value** (sum of proposal amounts × close probability)
- **Weekly close rate** (won / total closed)
- **Average deal size**
- **Days from first contact to close** (velocity)
- **Follow-up compliance** (% of prospects where next action is on time)

## Pipeline Health Warning Signs

- More than 30 deals in "REPLIED" but no calls booked → qualification bottleneck
- More than 10 deals in "PROPOSAL_SENT" with no closes in 2 weeks → proposal/pricing issue
- Same names sitting in pipeline > 30 days → dead leads, need pruning
