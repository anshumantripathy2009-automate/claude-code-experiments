# Discovery Call Playbook

**TL;DR:** 25-min structured conversation to (1) understand their business, (2) surface pain, (3) show they can trust you, (4) close for next step. Never pitch on discovery call — that's a follow-up move.

## Discovery Call Objectives

- Confirm they're a real ICP
- Understand their business in depth
- Surface the ₹ cost of their current pain
- Establish yourself as an expert
- Get them emotionally invested in solving the problem
- Close for a proposal (not for the sale)

## Discovery Call Structure (25 Minutes)

### Minute 0–3: Opening & Rapport
- Say hi, ask about their day
- Compliment something specific about their business/brand
- Set expectations: "This will be about 25 minutes. I'll ask questions first, then share what I think might help. Sound good?"

### Minute 3–8: Discover the Business
Questions:
- Tell me about your business — how did it start?
- How many customers do you serve per month?
- What's your ideal customer profile?
- What's working really well right now?

### Minute 8–15: Surface the Pain
Questions:
- Where does most of your business come from today?
- What frustrates you the most about your current growth?
- If you could snap your fingers and fix ONE thing, what would it be?
- How much do you think that problem costs you monthly? (In ₹ or customers)

**LISTEN. Do not interrupt. Take notes.**

### Minute 15–20: Explore the Vision
Questions:
- What does your business look like 12 months from now?
- What's stopping you from getting there?
- Have you tried any solutions? What worked / didn't work?

### Minute 20–23: Position the Solution
Do NOT pitch tiers or pricing. Say:

> "Based on what you shared, here's what I think would move the needle for you: [describe 2–3 specific outcomes tied to their pain]. I don't want to quote you on this call because I want to send you a proper proposal that fits your business. Is it okay if I send you something tomorrow?"

### Minute 23–25: Close for Proposal
- Confirm proposal delivery timeline
- Ask "What day this week works for a 15-min follow-up call to review it together?"
- Book the follow-up on the spot
- Thank them for their time

---

## Discovery Call Best Practices

### ✅ Do:
- **Take handwritten notes** (visible if on video)
- **Repeat their pain in their words** ("So you're saying...")
- **Use silence** — let them fill space, they'll reveal more
- **Confirm decision-maker status** ("Is this a decision you make solo or with someone else?")
- **Ask about budget indirectly** ("What have you spent on marketing in the past?")

### ❌ Don't:
- Pitch during discovery
- Talk more than 30% of the call
- Quote prices
- Send proposal on the call
- Argue or correct their assumptions
- Overexplain your services

---

## Discovery Call Red Flags (During the Call)

- They talk to you like a vendor, not a partner → probable low LTV
- They can't quantify their pain in ₹ → won't understand ROI later
- They interrupt constantly → will be a nightmare client
- They ask "how fast" repeatedly → looking for cheap + fast, low quality mindset
- Multiple people jump in the call → committee decision, long sales cycle
- They say "let me discuss with wife/husband/bhai" → not a true decision-maker

If 2+ red flags fire → disqualify politely at end, don't send proposal.

## Discovery Call Scorecard

Rate the call:
- [ ] They talked more than me (should be 70/30)
- [ ] I identified their #1 pain and quantified it
- [ ] I established authority via a specific insight they didn't have
- [ ] I booked the follow-up call before ending
- [ ] They're excited (visibly, verbally)
- [ ] Decision-maker confirmed
- [ ] Timeline confirmed
- [ ] Budget signal clear

5+ boxes checked = strong lead. Send proposal.
< 5 = weak lead. Send lite pitch + follow-up in 7 days.
