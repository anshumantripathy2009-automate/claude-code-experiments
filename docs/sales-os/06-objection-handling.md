# Objection Handling Playbook

**TL;DR:** Objections are buying signals, not rejection. Every "no" is a hidden question. Answer the question, don't argue the no. 30 common objections with responses below.

## Objection Handling Philosophy

1. **Never fight an objection.** Acknowledge it first.
2. **Reframe, don't argue.** Give them a new way to see it.
3. **Ask a question after every response.** Keep them talking.
4. **Silence after a price objection** = they'll often self-negotiate up.
5. **If they object 3+ times on the same thing** — they're not buying today. Move on politely.

---

## Universal Objection Handling Formula

**A — Acknowledge:** "Totally fair concern."
**R — Reframe:** "Here's another way to look at it..."
**E — Evidence:** "For example, [client/data]."
**A — Ask:** "Does that make sense?"

## THE 30 MOST COMMON OBJECTIONS (Indian SMB Context)

### PRICE OBJECTIONS

**1. "Bahut mehnga hai."**
> "Samajh sakta hoon. Compared to what specifically? If you compare us to Wix DIY, yes we're more expensive. But Wix takes 40 hours of your time to build + no support. Our client's average setup pays for itself in 1 extra customer. What's ONE new customer worth to you?"

**2. "Kya guarantee hai?"**
> "Great question. 7-day money-back if you're not happy with the delivered site. Free unlimited revisions during build. And with Care Plan, ongoing SLA. That's more guarantee than any freelancer will offer."

**3. "Fiverr pe ₹1000 mein mil jaata hai."**
> "Sure, but you've probably heard the horror stories — ships in 30 days, no support after, uses templates. NoirFlow is a partnership: fast delivery, ongoing operations, real design. If you want the cheapest, Fiverr is your answer. If you want it to actually work, we are."

**4. "Discount do."**
> "Our prices are already at the floor for the quality we deliver. But if you're a founding client, I can offer 15% off in exchange for a video testimonial. Fair trade?"

**5. "Budget nahi hai abhi."**
> "Cool. When would be a better time to revisit? Also — what's your realistic budget for growing your business online in the next 3 months? Let me see if there's a smaller-scope option that fits."

### VALUE OBJECTIONS

**6. "Mera business chal raha hai without a website."**
> "Absolutely — you've built something without one, that's impressive. But right now you're leaving revenue on the table. How much MORE could you grow with a proper digital presence? Even 20% growth = ₹X more per month. Is that worth ₹10K one-time?"

**7. "Instagram is enough for me."**
> "Instagram is discovery. But when someone SERIOUS is considering booking with you, they Google you. If nothing shows up, or something ugly shows up — they book with your competitor. A website is your credibility layer."

**8. "Mere customers Google nahi karte."**
> "Are you sure? 80% of Indian customers Google a business before deciding. Even your regulars share your Google Maps with friends. A proper site 5x's your referral conversion."

**9. "My existing website is fine."**
> "Cool — is it bringing you bookings/inquiries every week? If yes, awesome, don't fix what's not broken. If not, we should talk. Can I take a look and share 3 specific improvements? No cost."

**10. "Templates se bana lete hain."**
> "Sure, and it'll look like every other business using that template. If you want to stand out, that doesn't work. But if you're okay looking like everyone else — Wix has great templates."

### TRUST OBJECTIONS

**11. "Aap kaun ho? Never heard of NoirFlow."**
> "Fair — we're a new agency. Here's our portfolio: [link]. Talk to our recent clients — happy to intro. Everyone was our first client at some point."

**12. "Age kitni hai aapki?"**
> "18. And that's exactly why our clients hire us — we're building with AI-native tools while older agencies still use WordPress. Look at the work — that's what matters."

**13. "Team kitne log ho?"**
> "Two founders currently. We deliver every project ourselves — no outsourcing, no juniors. That's why quality is guaranteed."

**14. "Reviews kahan hain?"**
> "Currently building our review base — that's why we offer first 5 clients a discount for video testimonials. You'd be an early NoirFlow client and get founder-level attention."

**15. "Portfolio dikhao."**
> "Absolutely — [smile-dental.vercel.app], [aurum-realty.vercel.app]. Both live sites. Want to see how they were built?"

### TIMELINE OBJECTIONS

**16. "Time nahi hai abhi."**
> "That's exactly WHY you need us. We handle everything — you just approve. Total time from you: 30 min for a call + reviewing designs. That's it."

**17. "Baad mein karenge."**
> "When exactly? Every month you delay = customers going to competitors. Let's book a specific follow-up date so it doesn't slip. When works — 2 weeks from now?"

**18. "Peak season nahi hai."**
> "Perfect — that's when we should build. Slow season = time to prepare so you MAXIMIZE peak season. Building during peak = missing opportunities."

### DECISION OBJECTIONS

**19. "Bhai/family se puchna hoga."**
> "Totally fair. What information do they need? Let me prepare a 1-pager you can share. When can we schedule a 15-min call with them?"

**20. "Think karke bata dunga."**
> "Sure. What specifically do you need to think about? Sometimes I can address it right now if it's a specific concern."

**21. "Compare karke dekh raha hoon."**
> "Smart. Who else are you comparing with? Let me share what makes NoirFlow different from each — genuinely, no bashing others."

### DELIVERY OBJECTIONS

**22. "5 din mein kaise banega?"**
> "Because we use AI-native tools + a proven framework. Traditional agencies take 30 days because of email chains and outsourcing. We're 2 founders, direct comms, AI-accelerated build. Speed is our unfair advantage."

**23. "Quality compromise nahi hoga?"**
> "See our portfolio — that IS our 5-day quality. Speed comes from tools, not shortcuts."

**24. "Changes karne padenge baad mein."**
> "That's what Care Plan is for. Unlimited-to-quota updates handled every month, 24hr response. You're never stuck."

### SUPPORT OBJECTIONS

**25. "Aap disappear ho jaoge delivery ke baad."**
> "Fair concern — most freelancers do. That's why every NoirFlow project includes a Care Plan — monthly recurring means we NEED to keep you happy. Skin in the game."

**26. "24/7 support hai?"**
> "24-hour response time on Care Pro, same-day on Care Elite. Not 24/7 emergency line — that would be dishonest for a 2-person team. But we're responsive humans, not a ticket system."

### CARE PLAN OBJECTIONS

**27. "Care Plan nahi chahiye."**
> "Every NoirFlow site includes ongoing operations — that's not optional. Without it, we can't guarantee updates, uptime, or support. If you want a build-and-abandon situation, we're not the right fit — you'd get better value from Wix DIY. If you want a system that keeps performing, Care Plan is essential."

**28. "1 saal ke liye pay karke chhod dunga."**
> "That's fine — but why not just pay monthly? Locks you in less. Unless you want the 2-months-free annual discount?"

### AI-SPECIFIC OBJECTIONS

**29. "AI wala buzzword lagta hai."**
> "Fair — most AI marketing is hype. What we mean by AI: we use tools that let us build faster, and offer optional AI features like WhatsApp chatbots. That's it. Real, not hype."

**30. "AI ChatGPT hi kar leta hai — aap kyun chahiye?"**
> "ChatGPT is a tool. We're the architects. You wouldn't build a house because you own a hammer. You need the builder. We deliver the finished thing, ChatGPT can't."

## Objection Response Rules

1. Never sound defensive
2. Always acknowledge before reframing
3. Use their words back to them
4. Follow every response with a question
5. If they object 3x on the same thing → they're not buying → move on
