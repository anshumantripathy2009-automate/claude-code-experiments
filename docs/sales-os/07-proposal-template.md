## Proposal Delivery Rules

1. Send within 24 hours of discovery call
2. Send via WhatsApp PDF (not email — Indian SMBs check WhatsApp faster)
3. Follow up within 2 hours: "Any questions on this? Happy to jump on a quick 10-min call."
4. Never send without a clear follow-up call booked
5. Keep it visual — proposals are 40% design, 60% content

## Proposal Follow-Up Sequence

If not signed within:

- **48 hours:** "Hey [Name], any thoughts on the proposal?"
- **5 days:** Send a Loom video walking through the proposal
- **10 days:** Send a short case study of similar client + soft ask
- **20 days:** "Closing the loop — should I follow up in a month, or is now not the right time?"
- **30 days:** Move to long-term nurture (monthly check-in)
