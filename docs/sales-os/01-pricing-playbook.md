# Pricing Playbook (Advanced Edition)

**TL;DR:** We sell website builds (₹4,999–₹29,999) + monthly Care Plans (₹999–₹3,999). Every deal includes both. Never quote first. Always frame value before price. Anchor high, negotiate down within the range.

## Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring
Never lead with your lowest price. Always mention the premium tier first, then work down. Example:
> "Our Premium clients pay ₹25,000. Standard is ₹12,000. Given what you described, I'd recommend Standard."
This makes ₹12,000 feel like a deal — even though it's 2.4x your Basic tier.

### Loss Aversion
Frame the cost of NOT buying, not just the cost of buying.
> "Every missed WhatsApp inquiry = ₹5,000 lost patient. This site pays for itself in 2 patients."

### Decoy Effect
Always present 3 tiers. Middle tier gets 70% of sales. Design the middle tier to be the obvious best value.

### Precision Pricing
₹4,999 converts better than ₹5,000. ₹18,999 better than ₹19,000. The brain reads the first digit.

## Website Setup Tiers

### 🥉 BASIC — ₹4,999 floor / ₹7,999 ceiling
- **Buyer:** Solo local business, no site or ugly free-tier site
- **Examples:** Home tuition, single-outlet cafe, small salon, freelance photographer
- **Deliverables:**
  - 1-page mobile-first website
  - WhatsApp CTA (primary conversion)
  - Google Maps embed
  - Contact info + hours
  - Deployed on Vercel with custom subdomain
  - 3-day delivery
- **Required Care Plan:** Care Starter (₹999/mo)
- **Positioning:** "Your basic web presence, done right"

### 🥈 STANDARD — ₹9,999 floor / ₹14,999 ceiling
- **Buyer:** Growing local business, wants credibility
- **Examples:** Multi-doctor clinic, established gym, boutique retail, coaching institute (100+ students)
- **Deliverables:**
  - 3-section responsive site (Home, Services, Contact)
  - Services grid with icons
  - Testimonials section (3+)
  - Contact section with WhatsApp + Google Maps
  - SEO basics (title tags, meta descriptions, Open Graph)
  - Custom domain setup guidance
  - 5-day delivery
- **Required Care Plan:** Care Pro (₹1,999/mo) — DEFAULT
- **Positioning:** "The website your business actually deserves"

### 🥇 PREMIUM — ₹18,999 floor / ₹29,999 ceiling
- **Buyer:** Luxury/HNI-facing, established brand, needs prestige
- **Examples:** Luxury real estate, cosmetic surgery, IVF clinic, premium interior designer, fine-dining, wealth advisor
- **Deliverables:**
  - Fully custom design (unique to their brand)
  - 5–7 sections including portfolio/property/case study gallery
  - Premium hero with subtle animations
  - Advanced SEO + Schema markup
  - Multi-lang WhatsApp support setup
  - Analytics dashboard
  - 7–10 day delivery
- **Required Care Plan:** Care Pro or Care Elite (₹3,999/mo)
- **Positioning:** "Your brand's digital flagship"

## Discount Rules

**Default:** No discounts. Ever.

**Exception 1 — First 5 Clients (Founder Discount):**
- Up to 15% off setup fee
- ONLY in exchange for:
  - Video testimonial (recorded within 30 days of delivery)
  - Case study rights (real numbers, real name)
  - Written 5-star Google review

**Exception 2 — Annual Care Prepay:**
- 2 months free if they prepay 12 months of Care Plan upfront
- Locks them in, boosts cash flow

**Never Discount:**
- Because they "know" you
- Because they say "budget kam hai" without offering value in return
- Because they're "friend of a friend"
- Because you're desperate to close
