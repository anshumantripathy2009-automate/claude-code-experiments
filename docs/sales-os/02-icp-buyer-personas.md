# ICP & Buyer Personas (Who We Sell To)

**TL;DR:** We sell to owner-operator Indian SMBs in 6 primary niches. Each niche has a specific pain, budget, and buying behavior. Match the pitch to the persona.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Universal Criteria

A NoirFlow ICP client is:
- Owner-operator (decision-maker is 1 person, not a committee)
- Business ≥ 1 year old (has cash flow)
- Location: India (Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities)
- No website OR terrible website (Wix free-tier, Justdial-style)
- Active on Instagram or WhatsApp (reachable)
- Serves customers who make purchase decisions via digital
- Revenue: ₹5L+ per month (can afford us)
- English or Hinglish comfort

**Anti-ICP (do NOT sell to):**
- Big corporates (long sales cycles, we can't compete)
- Businesses with < ₹2L/mo revenue (can't afford care plan)
- Multi-decision-maker orgs (family businesses with 3 uncles arguing)
- "I want it free" mindset
- Solopreneurs still figuring out their offer

## PERSONA 1: Dr. Priya, The Independent Dentist 🦷

- **Business:** Solo or 2-doctor dental clinic
- **Location:** Tier-2 city (Bhubaneswar, Indore, Jaipur, Kochi)
- **Age:** 32–45
- **Monthly revenue:** ₹4L–₹15L
- **Marketing today:** Justdial listing, WhatsApp forwards, Google Maps
- **Digital sophistication:** Low-medium

### Pain Points
- Missed calls = missed patients (₹5K–₹50K per patient lost)
- Instagram DMs unanswered → patients book with competitor
- No system to reduce no-shows (~30% of appointments)
- Doesn't stand out from other clinics in the area

### What They Care About
- ROI in patients per month
- Not sounding "corporate"
- Being seen as modern and trustworthy
- Family-friendly image

### Pitch Angle
> "Missed WhatsApp inquiries = ₹50,000/month in lost patients. We build clinics a website + WhatsApp system that captures every lead automatically. Setup in 5 days, ROI in 1 patient."

### Price Range
- Setup: ₹9,999 (Standard)
- Care Plan: Care Pro ₹1,999/mo
- 90-day upsell: WhatsApp AI Receptionist ₹15K

### Where to Find
- Google Maps → "dental clinic [city]"
- Instagram search: dental clinic hashtags
- Practo, Justdial listings without websites
