# Follow-Up Sequences

**TL;DR:** 80% of deals close on follow-up 3+. Most sellers give up after follow-up 1. NoirFlow will out-follow-up every competitor because we have a system.

## The Follow-Up Truth

Studies show:
- 44% of salespeople give up after 1 follow-up
- 80% of sales require 5+ touchpoints
- **Sellers who follow up 5x close 3x more than sellers who follow up 1x**

## The NoirFlow Follow-Up Rules

1. **Every "no response" is a follow-up trigger — not a rejection.**
2. **Each follow-up must add value, not just check in.**
3. **Vary channels:** DM → WhatsApp → Email → Voice note → Video → Meme
4. **Track every touchpoint** in the pipeline (doc 09).
5. **Cap at 6 follow-ups. Then move to nurture.**

---

## Follow-Up Sequence: Post-Cold-DM (No Reply)

**Day 2:** Follow-up #1 (soft nudge)
> "Hey [Name], following up on my earlier message — just wanted to make sure it didn't get buried. No worries if not interested. 🙏"

**Day 7:** Follow-up #2 (add value)
> "Hi [Name] — I built a quick 45-sec video showing exactly what we do: [Loom link]. In case it helps decide."

**Day 14:** Follow-up #3 (case study)
> "Quick share — one of our clients (dental clinic in Bhubaneswar) is getting 15 extra WhatsApp inquiries/week since we built their site. Thought you might find it interesting: [screenshot/link]."

**Day 21:** Follow-up #4 (breakup)
> "Not sure if you saw my earlier messages — should I stop reaching out, or is now just not the right time? Either answer is helpful. 🙏"

**Day 30:** Move to long-term nurture (monthly value share)

---

## Follow-Up Sequence: Post-Discovery-Call (No Proposal Signed)

**Day 1:** Send proposal + "Any questions?"

**Day 2:** Voice note walking through key sections
> "Hey [Name], just recorded a 2-min voice note explaining the numbers in the proposal — check WhatsApp." [voice note]

**Day 5:** Loom video visualizing the outcome
> "Made a quick 3-min Loom showing what your site could look like — [link]"

**Day 10:** Case study of similar client
> "Sharing this — a real estate agent in Bhubaneswar landed 2 deals in 30 days after we built theirs. Might be useful."

**Day 15:** Direct ask
> "Hey [Name], to plan our schedule — should I hold your slot for this month, or move you to next month?"

**Day 20:** Breakup message
> "Closing the loop on the proposal. If timing's off, no worries. Should I stop following up? Just let me know either way. 🙏"

**Day 30:** Move to long-term nurture

---

## Long-Term Nurture (Post-Follow-Up-6)

Add them to a monthly value blast:
- Case studies
- Industry insights
- Free tips
- New portfolio pieces
- "Just checking in" every 2 months

You want to be top-of-mind when they're finally ready to buy.

---

## Value-Add Ideas (For Every Follow-Up)

Never send "just checking in." Every follow-up must give something:

- Portfolio piece relevant to their industry
- Screenshot of a similar client's win
- 30-sec voice note breaking down their competitor's site
- Article/video that helps their business
- Personal observation about their Instagram or business
- A free micro-audit ("Noticed your site takes 8 sec to load — that's costing you customers")

## Follow-Up Frequency Cheat Sheet

| Stage | Frequency | Duration |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Cold outbound no reply | Every 3–7 days | Max 4 touchpoints |
| Post-discovery no proposal signed | Every 2–5 days | Max 6 touchpoints |
| Long-term nurture | Monthly | Forever |
| Won client | Bi-weekly value | Ongoing relationship |

---

## Message Timing Best Practices

- **10 AM–1 PM IST:** morning phone-check window
- **6 PM–9 PM IST:** evening phone-check window
- **NEVER** send Sunday morning or after 10 PM
- **Weekdays** > weekends for B2B
