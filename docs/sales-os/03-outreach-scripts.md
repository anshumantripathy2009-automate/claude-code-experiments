# Outreach Scripts (DM + WhatsApp + Email)

**TL;DR:** Every outreach is personalized in the first line, valuable in the middle, and clear on the ask at the end. Never send generic templates. Reference the persona-specific scripts below.

## Rules of Cold Outreach

1. **First line = personalization proof.** Reference something specific to their business.
2. **Second line = pain acknowledged.** Show you understand their world.
3. **Third line = value offered.** Show, don't sell (send portfolio link).
4. **Fourth line = soft ask.** Never "book a call." Ask a question they can answer with 1 word.
5. **Never longer than 5 lines.** Cold DMs die in paragraphs.
6. **Never emoji spam.** 1 emoji max per DM.
7. **Send between 10 AM–1 PM or 6 PM–9 PM IST** (business owner phone-check windows).

## REPLY HANDLERS

### They reply "how much?"
> "Depends on what you need — ranges from ₹5K to ₹30K. Can I ask 2 quick questions about your business first so I can give you a real answer?"

### They reply "we already have a website"
> "Gotcha — is it working for you? Meaning, is it actually bringing bookings/inquiries every week? If yes, awesome. If not, happy to take a look and share thoughts (free)."

### They reply "interested — tell me more"
> "Great. Instead of me pitching, can I ask 3 quick questions? Then I'll tell you if we're a fit."
>
> Q1: What does your business do exactly?
> Q2: How do most customers find you today?
> Q3: What's the #1 growth problem right now?

### They reply "budget kam hai"
> "Total samajh sakta hoon. Basic tier is ₹4,999 with 3-day delivery. But instead of guessing, can I ask what your monthly marketing budget is? Then I'll only pitch what makes sense."

### They reply with insults or spam
> Don't respond. Block if needed. Move on. Your energy is expensive.
