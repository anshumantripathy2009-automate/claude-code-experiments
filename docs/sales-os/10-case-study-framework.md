# Case Study Framework

**TL;DR:** Every won client = a sales asset. Structure their win as a case study within 30 days of delivery. Use it in future outreach. Compounds forever.

## Why Case Studies Matter

- 3x higher close rate on prospects who see relevant case studies
- Trust proxy for a new agency
- Reusable content forever
- Basis for referrals and repeat sales

## Case Study Structure (1-Page PDF)

---

**[Client Name] Case Study — NoirFlow**

[Business name, industry, city, brief description]

[What was broken before NoirFlow]

- Pain point 2

## Case Study Rules

1. **Only real numbers.** Never inflate. One inflated case study kills the brand.
2. **Get written approval** from client before publishing.
3. **Anonymize if needed** ("A dental clinic in Bhubaneswar" if client prefers).
4. **Update every 90 days** with fresh metrics.
5. **Store in `/docs/case-studies/[client-name].md`**

## Case Study Collection Process

**Day 15 after delivery:**
- Ask client: "What's changed since we launched?"
- Collect metrics: WhatsApp inquiries, calls, bookings, revenue

**Day 30 after delivery:**
- Formal metrics collection
- Written testimonial request
- Video testimonial (if founding client)

**Day 45:**
- Publish case study PDF
- Add to prospect outreach toolkit
- Share on Instagram/LinkedIn with client's permission

## Case Study Distribution

- Include in every proposal to similar-industry prospects
- Post to Instagram/LinkedIn (with client tag)
- Send in follow-up sequences as "value-add"
- Feature on future NoirFlow site's "Work" section

## Anti-Patterns (Never Do)

- Made-up numbers
- Fake testimonials with stock photos (kills credibility)
- Publishing without client approval
- Overpromising future results
